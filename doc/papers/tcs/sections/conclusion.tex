\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We have formalized in Lean~4, as part of CSLib, Crole's result that
several definitions of $\alpha$-equivalence coincide. We found no
errors in Crole's proofs, and the formalization supplies the arguments
he omits (Table~\ref{tab:gaps}). Following Norrish and Vestergaard, we
have also formalized an isomorphism between the quotient of named terms
by $\alpha$-equivalence and pure de Bruijn terms, with their respective
notions of $\beta$-reduction. Through it, the Church--Rosser theorem
for de Bruijn terms yields the Church--Rosser theorem for named terms
modulo $\alpha$-equivalence (Corollary~\ref{cor:church-rosser}).
Together these results connect two of the standard approaches to
variable binding within a single library. As part of CSLib, they are
reviewed and kept up to date with Mathlib, so that later work can
depend on them directly. They can be extended by further binding
representations and by the remaining relationships of the network of
Norrish and Vestergaard (Figure~\ref{fig:equivalence-net}).
