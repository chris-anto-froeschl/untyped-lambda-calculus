\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We have formalized in Lean~4, as part of CSLib, Crole's result that
five definitions of alpha equivalence coincide and, following Norrish
and Vestergaard, an isomorphism between the quotient of named terms by
alpha equivalence and pure de Bruijn terms. Together these results
connect two of the standard approaches to variable binding within a
single library. They are meant to be maintained as part of CSLib
rather than as a standalone development: subject to code review, kept
up to date with CSLib and Mathlib, and structured so that later
projects can depend on them directly. They are the starting point of a
foundation for research on the untyped lambda calculus, to be extended
by further binding representations and the remaining relationships of
Figure~\ref{fig:equivalence-net}, and to serve in teaching the lambda
calculus.
